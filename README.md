# ClothingStartups

### Textile knowledge, clothing business insights, and free production planning tools

**[Visit ClothingStartups](https://keqiaoguide.com/)**

ClothingStartups is an English-language platform for people building clothing businesses and sourcing fabrics. It connects the decisions that are often handled separately: who a garment is for, which material suits it, how it will be produced, and whether the first order makes financial sense.

The platform serves independent clothing brands, fashion studios, fabric sourcing teams, small online sellers, and founders preparing their first collection. Its textile coverage draws on the practical questions found around Keqiao, China, while its clothing business coverage is written for readers worldwide.

This repository introduces the website and provides a starting point for its public resources. It is a project overview, rather than a distribution of the website's application source code.

## Two connected areas: textiles and clothing

### Textiles: understand the material before placing the order

A fabric name alone rarely answers a buyer's real questions. Composition, construction, usable width, finished weight, pretreatment, dyeing, finishing, and order quantity can all change the result.

ClothingStartups covers topics such as:

- Fabric families and their garment applications.
- The difference between greige fabric and an approved finished material.
- Dyeing quotations, finishing requirements, and small-order cost allocation.
- Stretch, hand feel, appearance, and the effect of different processing routes.
- Knitted fabric defects, crease marks, inspection, and production disputes.
- Market developments that affect sourcing decisions.

Browse [Textiles](https://keqiaoguide.com/textiles/), [Fabric Guides](https://keqiaoguide.com/fabric-guides/), [Dyeing & Finishing](https://keqiaoguide.com/dyeing-finishing/), and [Sourcing](https://keqiaoguide.com/sourcing/).

### Clothing: turn a product idea into a workable first order

Starting a brand involves more than designing a logo or choosing a trending product. Founders need to decide who they can reach, what they can afford to develop, which production model suits their budget, and how much inventory they can carry.

The clothing section examines:

- Choosing a customer group and a focused first product.
- Comparing private label, blanks and decoration, and custom cut-and-sew routes.
- Planning a first drop and controlling inventory exposure.
- Samples, minimum order quantities, manufacturing briefs, and supplier quotations.
- Online selling, distribution, and the relationship between product costs and retail pricing.
- Brand case studies and competitive changes in the apparel market.

Start with [How to Start a Clothing Brand](https://keqiaoguide.com/articles/how-to-start-a-clothing-brand/) or explore the [Clothing archive](https://keqiaoguide.com/clothing/).

## Free tools

### 1. Garment Cost and Fabric Consumption Calculator

**[Open the garment cost calculator](https://keqiaoguide.com/tools/garment-cost-calculator/)**

Enter a target number of finished garments, fabric consumption per piece, cutting waste, and sampling reserve to estimate the meters of fabric to purchase. Then add your quoted fabric price, trims, sewing, decoration, packaging, inspection, freight allowance, overhead, and one-time setup costs.

The calculator returns:

- Fabric needed before waste.
- Cutting and process waste allowance.
- Total fabric purchase estimate, rounded up to a full meter.
- Estimated production cost for the order.
- Estimated cost per garment.
- A selling price based on your chosen gross margin.

For example, 500 T-shirts at 1.3 meters per piece, with 8% waste and a 6-meter sampling reserve, produce a purchase estimate of **708 meters**:

```text
500 × 1.3 × 1.08 + 6 = 708 meters
```

Consumption presets are editable starting points. Prices are entered by the user; the tool does not retrieve live quotations or convert currencies. Final fabric requirements should be confirmed with the size mix, usable width, shrinkage, and cutting marker.

### 2. Fabric Composition and Construction Guide

**[Open the fabric comparison tool](https://keqiaoguide.com/tools/fabric-finder/)**

Search fabric families, filter by intended product, and compare up to three materials. The guide covers cotton jersey, French terry, sweatshirt fleece, interlock, nylon–spandex knit, polyester performance knit, satin, stretch crepe, viscose twill, denim, linen, and synthetic suede.

Comparison details include fiber family, construction, typical hand, and the checks to make before ordering. Product filters include T-shirts, hoodies, activewear, dresses, trousers, and home textiles.

The guide helps narrow a sourcing conversation. It does not replace an approved swatch or a supplier's exact specification.

### 3. Factory RFQ Builder

**[Build a clothing or fabric supplier inquiry](https://keqiaoguide.com/tools/rfq-builder/)**

Prepare an editable request for quotation with product type, quantity, destination market, fabric requirements, colors, sizes, decoration, trims, delivery targets, budget, and sample requirements.

The generated inquiry also asks suppliers to confirm minimums, sample costs, lead times, price inclusions, inspection arrangements, and payment and delivery terms.

Copy the finished brief or download it as a text file. The tool prepares the inquiry locally in the browser; it does not send it to a factory.

## Services

The platform presents three areas of sourcing and production planning support:

### Fabric sourcing

Turn a garment application, target hand, reference material, and budget into a clearer fabric brief. The service scope includes specification checklists, stock versus custom options, sampling and testing plans, and comparable supplier inquiries.

[Explore fabric sourcing](https://keqiaoguide.com/services/fabric-sourcing/).

### Production sourcing

Prepare a manufacturing request that fits the product, quantity, and development stage. Compare production routes, manufacturer capabilities, quotation terms, and sample approval requirements.

[Explore production sourcing](https://keqiaoguide.com/services/factory-matching/).

### First-drop planning

Work through the first product, development budget, sampling costs, and inventory exposure before placing a bulk order.

[Explore first-drop planning](https://keqiaoguide.com/services/startup-consulting/).

The current [project request page](https://keqiaoguide.com/contact/) helps prepare and download a brief. Email submission is not yet connected. Supplier availability, acceptance, and project terms require individual confirmation.
